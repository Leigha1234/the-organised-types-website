Before you get in touch

A few useful details.

What does the £149 setup fee cover?

An initial business review and up to two hours configuring one agreed workflow in TOTS-OS. Larger data imports, migrations or additional setup are quoted separately.

What can I use the two support hours for?

Agreed small website edits, templates or improvements to a business process. The two hours are a total shared allowance across Sam and Leigha, separate from your planning session. We agree priorities first and quote any additional work before starting it.

Are full website builds or marketing management included?

No. Full website builds, custom software development, ongoing social media management and advertising spend are separate services. We can discuss those and provide a separate quote.

Do unused hours roll over?

No. The practical support allowance is two hours each month and unused hours do not roll over.

Is this unlimited support?

No. The package includes the planning session, action plan, two practical support hours, TOTS-OS access and one mid-month email check-in. Extra work or support is scoped and agreed separately.

Which TOTS-OS plan is included?

We'll confirm the included plan and its features in your written proposal before you subscribe, so you know exactly what you're receiving.

How do I get started?

Send an enquiry through our contact form and mention Monthly Business Support. We'll talk through what you need, confirm the scope and cancellation terms, and send the details before you commit.

What's been sitting on your to-do list?

Tell us what you're trying to move forward. We'll help you work out whether monthly support or a specific project is the right next step.
